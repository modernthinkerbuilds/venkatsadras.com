Where do we eat?

Every family asks it three times a day. I built an agent to answer it, took it on a family trip to New York and Boston before it was ready, asked every question twice, and logged the difference. Each episode opens on a meal and closes on one thing anyone running agents has to get right. The series is ongoing.
