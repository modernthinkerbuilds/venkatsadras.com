The work

At home I build and run my own agents: Miya, my personal AI agent, and its team, on Rahat, the platform underneath them. They run in my family's life every day, which means I feel every failure the same day.

I care about agents you'd actually point at your own life: ones that remember, coordinate, check themselves, and know when not to act. Most of what goes wrong with agents isn't in the model. It's in the layer underneath, and that's the layer I like to build.

Off the keyboard

  • Dad of two. Much of Rahat was built on paternity leave, with our youngest supervising most of it.
  • CrossFit. My reset. It's also why two agents on the team exist: Fraser and Huberman.
  • Learning the guitar. Slowly. A good reminder of what it's like to be a beginner.
  • Food and travel. Which is how a food agent ended up on a family trip to New York and Boston, before it was ready. That's the series.

Underneath all of it: as much time with family as I can get, having fun and living life. The agents are there to give time back, not take it.
