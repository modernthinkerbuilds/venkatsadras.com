Rahat, the agent platform I built

The platform, not each agent, owns the loop an answer runs through, called the harness. Two of the seven agents answer through it today:

  1. UnderstandTurns my question into a typed request.
  2. Set the barWrites down what a good answer must meet.
  3. Do the workSearches and computes, under a spending limit.
  4. Check in codeEvery claim is checked against its evidence.
  5. Second opinionAnother AI grades taste. It has no vote yet.
  6. Fix or say soOne repair, or it says plainly what failed.
  7. Fill the numbersEvery number comes from a record, not the AI.
  8. Keep a recordThe run, the checks and the cost are saved.

86 real questions from my family are kept as private tests that re-run for $0, and the code has 4,209 automated tests (Oct 17). It's open source.
