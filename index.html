A rule in a prompt is a request. A check in the path is a guarantee.

An agent can be fluent and wrong at the same time, and you can't tell from reading it. Run more than one and they contradict each other, act at moments they shouldn't, and remember the wrong things. Those problems don't live in the model. They live in the layer underneath it. That layer is what I build.
