The rewrite that mattered

A few weeks in, Miya told me a fact about me that had never been true, in a flawless voice. My first instinct was the wrong one: I went to the prompt. Ten patches later I was treating a systemic failure as a run of bad luck, so I asked which layer of the system was supposed to catch this. Nothing was there.

I stopped adding features and rebuilt the core: the facts on record about me go in front of the model, and a validator sits between the agent and me. A rule in a prompt is a request. A check in code, on the way out, runs every time.

Then I replayed 126 real messages from my own history. Internal-voice leaks went to 0%, and about one reply in twenty had a contradiction the validator caught and corrected.
